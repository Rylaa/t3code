# Focus mode

Give a project a deadline and a note about what is left to do, then turn on focus
mode so only that work can interrupt you.

## Focus a project

Click the target button at the top of the sidebar and pick a project under **Focus
on a project**. Choose a deadline (today, tomorrow, or Friday at 18:00, or your own
date and time) and write what is left to do. Pick the project again, or click it in
the **Focus** section, to change the deadline or remove the focus. Agents can set it
too through `t3_project_update`.

## Turn focus mode on or off

Use the **Focus mode** switch in the same menu, or press `mod+alt+f`. Like a system
focus mode, it applies to the device where you turn it on.

While it is on:

- Focused projects and their threads move to a **Focus** section at the top of the
  sidebar, nearest deadline first. The deadline turns amber on its last day and red
  once it passes. Snoozed and settled threads stay on their own shelves.
- A focused thread that needs an answer or approval blinks until you respond, and its
  notification stays on screen until you open or answer the thread. While the app is
  in the background, the desktop app also bounces its Dock icon on macOS.
- Completions and every other thread stay quiet.

Turn it off to return to your normal notifications.

## Reminders and Calendar

On macOS, the desktop app adds each focused project's deadline and note to a **T3
Code** list in Reminders and a **T3 Code** calendar in Calendar, whether or not focus
mode is on. macOS asks for access the first time. Removing a focus removes its
reminder and event.
